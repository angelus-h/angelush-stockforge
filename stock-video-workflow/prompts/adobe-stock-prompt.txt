1. [VISUAL_ANALYSIS]: Details of what happens in the video (subject, action, framing, lighting, colors).

### OUTPUT FORMAT:
You must respond strictly in JSON format with exactly two keys: "title" and "comma_separated_keywords". Do not include any markdown wrappers or additional text outside the JSON block.
