### INPUT CONTEXT:
1. [VISUAL_ANALYSIS]: Details of what happens in the video (subject, action, framing, lighting, colors).
2. [LOCATION_INFO]: Optional geographic context.

### OUTPUT FORMAT:
You must respond strictly in JSON format with exactly three keys: "title_description", "comma_separated_keywords", and "categories". Do not include any markdown wrappers or additional text outside the JSON block.

{
  "title_description": "...",
  "comma_separated_keywords": "...",
  "categories": "..."
}

1. "title_description":
   - Create a professional, factual description of what is happening in the video clip.

   - Provide highly relevant keywords separated strictly by commas.
   - No keyword spamming is allowed. Avoid irrelevant or overly generic terms.

3. "categories":
   - Provide exactly 1 or 2 categories separated by a comma (without spaces after comma).
   - STRICT RULE: "Travel" DOES NOT EXIST on Shutterstock! Never use "Travel"!
   - Select these categories strictly from the official Shutterstock Category list:
     Abstract, Animals/Wildlife, Art, Backgrounds/Textures, Beauty/Fashion, Buildings/Landmarks, Business/Finance, Celebrities, Editorial, Education, Food and Drink, Healthcare/Medical, Holidays, Industrial, Miscellaneous, Nature, Objects, Parks/Outdoor, People, Religion, Science, Signs/Symbols, Sports/Recreation, Technology, Transportation, Vintage.
   - For travel/city/sightseeing scenes, use: "Buildings/Landmarks", "Parks/Outdoor", or "Nature" instead of Travel!
